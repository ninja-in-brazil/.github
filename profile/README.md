# Ninja in Brazil 🥷

**Personal development for developers who train beyond code.**

Most developer tools stop at syntax, tickets, and frameworks. **Ninja in Brazil** is an open-source ecosystem and training ground for software engineers who want to build across mind, body, and craft.

[Website](https://ninjainbrazil.com/) · [The Book](https://ninjainbrazil.com/book) · [Ninja Track](https://ninjainbrazil.com/track) · [Display Profile](https://ninjainbrazil.com/display) · [Blog](https://blog.ninjainbrazil.com/)

---

## Why Train Beyond Code?

Raw coding skills are no longer enough. The engineers who stand out think across disciplines, adapt under pressure, and debug both systems and themselves.

* **Not another productivity hack:** No rigid streaks, notification overload, or gamified to-do lists that burn out your focus.
* **Cross-disciplinary leverage:** Connect software engineering with martial arts, music, fitness, art, and intentional living spaces.
* **Sustainable growth:** Weekly goal flows, brain-centric habit loops, and tools designed for long-term compounding.

## Featured Book: *Modern Applications of Ninjutsu*

> "Ancient wisdom meets modern software engineering. Learn how to think like a ninja in the modern world of tech, startups, and career growth."

Written by [Leonid Medovyy](https://github.com/leouofa), [*Modern Applications of Ninjutsu*](https://ninjainbrazil.com/book) is not a historical text. It is a practical field manual that adapts adaptability, perseverance, strategic thinking, and continuous self-improvement to modern software careers.

**What the 12 chapters cover:**

* **Mind & Strategy:** *The Modern Day Ninja*, *Learning What Matters*, and *Make a Plan* for high-leverage skill selection and burnout-free focus.
* **Body & Energy:** *Food for Ninjas* and *Fitness for Ninjas* to sustain deep intellectual work.
* **Craft & Creativity:** *Tech for Ninjas*, *Art for Ninjas*, *Music for Ninjas*, and *Engineering for Ninjas* for building resilient systems and entering flow state.
* **Environment & People:** *Social Skills for Ninjas*, *Plants for Ninjas*, and *Living Spaces for Ninjas* for navigating teams and designing productive surroundings.

**[Read the Open-Source Book Preview on GitHub](https://github.com/ninja-in-brazil/modern-applications-of-ninjutsu)** · **[Get the Full Book](https://ninjainbrazil.com/book)**

## Open-Source Repositories

Every repository in this organization supports a specific pillar of the modern developer's training:

* **[`modern-applications-of-ninjutsu`](https://github.com/ninja-in-brazil/modern-applications-of-ninjutsu)**
  Open-source preview of *Modern Applications of Ninjutsu*. Explore the core mental models, engineering strategies, and chapters directly on GitHub.

* **[`ninja-track`](https://github.com/ninja-in-brazil/ninja-track)** (`TypeScript` · `MIT`)
  A mobile habit tracker built to reduce decision fatigue. Uses weekly goal flows, flexible tag-based logging, and a brain-centric reward loop so habits stick without the overwhelm. ([Learn more](https://ninjainbrazil.com/track))

* **[`ninja-coach`](https://github.com/ninja-in-brazil/ninja-coach)** (`TypeScript`)
  An AI-powered top performance coach designed to help you establish, refine, and sustain high-performance daily routines.

* **[`super-dotfiles`](https://github.com/ninja-in-brazil/super-dotfiles)** (`Shell`)
  *Get Super.* An ergonomic macOS terminal and development environment that treats the CLI as an instrument, helping you get sharper every time you sit at the keyboard.

## The Ninja in Brazil Platform

Alongside our open-source repositories, [ninjainbrazil.com](https://ninjainbrazil.com/) offers free web tools to tie your training together:

* **[Track](https://ninjainbrazil.com/track):** Set weekly goals, log activities across any discipline, and visualize progress without rigid streak anxiety.
* **[Display](https://ninjainbrazil.com/display):** Turn your growth into a public developer profile ("your dojo mirror") with Markdown support, AI-assisted project blurbs, and custom domain hosting without watermarks.
